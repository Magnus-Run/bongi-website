The roadmap

Where we are investing.

North Invent's engineering roadmap is a set of deliberate bets on where the bridge is going. Each one is funded, structured, and in progress.

01

WaveLINK — Satellite Safety Communications

Developed with Thales and launched in Q2 2026, WaveLINK is a retrofit satellite safety communications terminal. It moves North Invent from display supplier into a more consequential part of the bridge.

02

Fostech — Maritime Augmented Reality

North Invent holds a 20% stake in Fostech, a company developing augmented reality technology for maritime navigation. A financial commitment to the technology most likely to change how navigators interact with the bridge.

03

R&D at Aalborg University, Copenhagen

Our R&D office is embedded at Aalborg University in Copenhagen. That placement is deliberate. It keeps our engineering work close to the research shaping the next generation of bridge technology.

M-AR

Navigation, ten years from now.

North Invent is developing the navigation systems that the industry has not yet standardised. M-AR, the company's Maritime Augmented Reality project, overlays chart data, vessel position, and environmental information directly onto the operator's field of view, keeping eyes on the water without a separate screen to consult.

Most bridge hardware today requires the operator to shift attention between the environment and the screen. M-AR removes that split. It is not a concept; the prototype has been demonstrated on a real vessel bridge, with a full navigational overlay in operation.
