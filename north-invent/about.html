Twenty years of
engineering discipline.

The bridge has changed more in the past two decades than in the fifty years before it.

It has moved from a room of analogue instruments to a fully digital, networked command environment. That shift is not complete. S-100 chart standards, autonomous vessel systems, and augmented-reality navigation are converging on a bridge that will look substantially different from today's within a decade.

North Invent understood this early. The company was founded in 2004 with a specific read on the market: that the maritime industry needed display hardware engineered to the demands of the bridge, not adapted from consumer or industrial electronics. That meant building to IEC 60945 from the outset, designing mounting systems for a confined and safety-critical working environment, and accounting for a vessel service life measured in decades, not product cycles.

That discipline produced the Wave II: a touchscreen SMART monitor with a patented mounting system that allows installation on a table, ceiling, or flush into a console. The Wave II is built into Wärtsilä's NACOS Platinum integrated bridge system. It won a Red Dot Design Award in 2023. More than 20,000 units are in service. Those are not incidental outcomes. They follow directly from the engineering choices made at the start.

Every significant decision since has followed the same logic. WaveLINK, launched with Thales in Q2 2026, extends the company's capability into satellite safety communications: a part of the bridge where the hardware requirements are more demanding and the consequences of failure are greater. A 20% stake in Fostech places North Invent inside the development of augmented-reality technology for maritime navigation. An R&D office at Aalborg University in Copenhagen provides the engineering depth to pursue both.

The pattern is consistent across twenty years. North Invent builds hardware for where the bridge is now and invests in the technology that will define where it goes next. That is not a strategy statement. It is the record.

The roadmap

Where we are going.

Three investments define North Invent's direction over the next decade. Each one is active, funded, and in progress.

01

WaveLINK: Satellite Safety Communications

Launched with Thales in Q2 2026, WaveLINK is a retrofit satellite safety communications terminal. It takes North Invent from display supplier to a broader role in the bridge's safety infrastructure.

02

Fostech: Maritime Augmented Reality

Fostech is developing augmented-reality technology for maritime navigation. North Invent holds a 20% stake. That is not an observation about a trend. It is a financial position inside the technology most likely to reshape how navigators interact with their instruments.

03

R&D at Aalborg University, Copenhagen

The R&D office at Aalborg University in Copenhagen underpins both. It is the company's long-term engineering capability, embedded in a university research environment and oriented towards the bridge of the next generation.
