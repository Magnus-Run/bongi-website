How I work

From first conversation
to finished work.

The first conversation is not a sales call. I am not going to pitch you a package or ask about your budget. I want to understand your product, your market, and where the communication is breaking down. In most engagements, the stated problem is not the real one. That gap is where the work actually begins.

Once I have a clear picture, I put together a diagnosis. What is working, what is not, and why. From there, we agree on a scope and I get to work. No juniors, no account managers, no handoffs. You work with me directly, from the first conversation to the final deliverable.

The output is usable, not decorative. A communication platform your leadership team can operate from. A pitch that holds up when the hard questions come. A message architecture your marketing team can build on independently. I do not produce frameworks that need a workshop to interpret.

Some clients engage me for a single project. A funding round, a product launch, a market entry. Others keep me on to work alongside their team over time. Either way, you always know what you are getting and from whom.

Common questions

What people
usually ask

What does a strategic communication advisor actually do?

I work with technical founders and B2B tech companies to close the gap between what they have built and how they communicate it. That means uncovering the real narrative, structuring it into a clear logical framework, and giving the leadership team or marketing function the language to operate from independently. The output is not a campaign. It is the foundation that makes all subsequent communication coherent.

How is working with Bongi different from hiring an agency?

With an agency, you get a team you did not hire, managed by an account manager who does not fully understand your product, executing a brief they half-understood. With me, you get one person. 20 years of experience, full attention, no handoffs. I do the work myself, from diagnosis to delivery.

What kind of companies do you work with?

Technical founders preparing to raise, B2B tech companies that have traction but no clear voice, and leadership teams facing a high-stakes external moment. A funding round, a government tender, a strategic partnership. The common thread is a strong product and a communication gap that is costing them.

How long does an engagement typically take?

It depends on the scope. A focused narrative architecture project, where we define positioning and build the communication platform, typically takes four to six weeks. A pitch development engagement, working towards a specific external moment, can be shorter. I do not pad timelines and I do not drag out work to fill a retainer.

Where do you start when you take on a new client?

With the problem, not the brief. Before I propose anything, I spend time understanding where the real friction is. Whether that is a pitch that is not landing, a narrative that is too vague, or a team that has no consistent language to work from. The first conversation is diagnostic. I listen more than I speak.

Do you work with companies outside Norway?

Yes. I work with founders and companies across Europe and beyond. Most engagements are conducted remotely, so location is not a constraint. If you have built something technically strong and need help making it legible to the audiences that matter, geography is the least of your concerns.
